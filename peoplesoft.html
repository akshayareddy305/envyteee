FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you upgrade us from an older PeopleSoft release to 9.2?

Yes. After assessing your customizations and integrations, we plan a phased upgrade with regression testing before the production cutover.

How long does a PeopleSoft upgrade take?

It depends on the release gap, the volume of customizations and your integrations. After the assessment, we provide a timeline estimate.

Can PeopleSoft run on Oracle Cloud Infrastructure?

Yes. PeopleSoft can be deployed on OCI, and Cloud Manager helps automate patching and environment management.

What is selective adoption?

Selective adoption means choosing which new PeopleSoft features to activate from each Oracle update, instead of taking everything, so changes stay low-risk and valuable.

Do you offer ongoing support after go-live?

Yes. We offer managed services with defined SLAs across all environments.

Do you work with HCM, Finance and Campus Solutions?

Yes. Our team supports all three application families.
